# Steering — decisions from the human

The human writes this file from interactive sessions. Each entry is a decision about strategy,
with its reasoning. Treat an active entry as binding until the human retires it. Don't edit this
file: if you disagree with an entry, or the evidence turns against it, say so in your journal (and
in a `needs-human` issue if it matters), and keep following it meanwhile.

When a new entry appears, apply it to `state/` in your next run, and acknowledge it in that day's
journal (or the weekly one) by its id: what you changed, or why nothing needed changing.
